| **### Question 1 - Purchasing Options for SageMaker Inference**<br><br>An e-commerce company runs a stable, year-round fraud detection model on Amazon SageMaker AI real-time endpoints. Any usage beyond the committed amount ($50/hour in this case) is billed at the standard On-Demand rate.
