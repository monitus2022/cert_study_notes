A large retail company is exploring generative AI to enhance its customer service chatbot. The company aims to fine-tune models from third-party services to enhance the chatbot’s comprehension of business-specific customer inquiries.

Which benefits of Amazon Bedrock will help the retail company meet its requirements? (Select TWO.)

* **A)** Offers distinct APIs for accessing different foundation models (FMs).
* **B)** Amazon Bedrock enables private customization of foundation models (FMs).
* **C)** Ensures that prompts and responses remain completely private and secure.
* **D)** Offers a wide range of pre-trained foundation models (FMs) from multiple providers.
* **E)** Amazon Bedrock requires customers to handle infrastructure provisioning.

??? note "Click to reveal Answer & Explanation"
    **Correct Answer:** **B and D**

    #### Correct Option Analysis
    * **B is Correct:** Amazon Bedrock enables private customization of foundation models, allowing the retail company to fine-tune third-party models using its own business-specific data. This directly supports improving the chatbot’s comprehension of customer inquiries.
    * **D is Correct:** Amazon Bedrock provides access to a wide range of pre-trained foundation models from multiple providers. This helps the company evaluate and use third-party generative AI models for its chatbot.

    #### Distractor Analysis
    * **A is Incorrect:** Amazon Bedrock provides a unified, serverless experience for accessing multiple foundation models. It does not require learning distinct APIs for each model provider.
    * **C is Incorrect:** While Amazon Bedrock offers strong privacy and security controls, the statement that it ensures prompts and responses remain completely private and secure is overly absolute. More importantly, the primary requirements here are multi-provider access and private fine-tuning, not security alone.
    * **E is Incorrect:** Amazon Bedrock is a managed service and does **not** require customers to provision or manage underlying infrastructure.

    > **Key Takeaway:** Amazon Bedrock helps teams adopt generative AI by providing **access to multiple third-party FMs** and by supporting **private customization/fine-tuning** of those models using business-specific data.
