# Skill 2.1.6: Assess Tradeoffs Between ML Model Performance, Training Time, and Cost
## Task 2.1: ML Model and Foundation Model (FM) Development - Choose appropriate modeling approaches for ML and AI solutions

> **Core Principle:** Model selection balances **performance**, **training time**, and **cost** against hard constraints (**interpretability, latency, compliance**). No single optimum—match the approach to the business requirement.

### Core Tradeoff Triangle
- **Performance:** Accuracy, precision/recall, F1, AUC, RMSE. Driven by data quality, feature richness, algorithm complexity, and tuning.
- **Training Time:** Data prep, feature engineering, training compute, hyperparameter tuning, retraining cadence. Often **>80% of total effort**.
- **Cost:** Training compute, storage, labeling, inference hosting, operational overhead. **Training is one-time; inference scales with traffic.**
- **Hard Constraints:** Interpretability, latency, compliance, security. These can **override raw performance** (e.g., finance rejects black-box models).

### Decision Matrix: Prioritize by Requirement
| Use Case Profile | Prioritize | Sacrifice | AWS Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real-time fraud detection | Low latency, high recall | Training time, cost | XGBoost, SageMaker real-time endpoint |
| Weekly demand forecast | Interpretability, cost | Training time | SageMaker built-in linear/regression, Batch Transform |
| Medical imaging diagnosis | High accuracy | Cost, training time | Deep learning, GPU, SageMaker training |
| High-volume text classification | Cost, latency | Some accuracy | Distilled model, Serverless Inference |
| Rapid prototyping | Training time | Cost, performance | SageMaker JumpStart, AutoGluon, managed AI |
| Fixed business logic | Simplicity, auditability | N/A | Plain code rules |

### Levers & Tradeoffs
| Lever | Performance | Training Time | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| More data | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ | Diminishing returns, overfitting risk |
| More features | ↑ then ↓ | ↑ | ↑ | Feature engineering cost |
| Complex algorithm | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ | Black box, harder to explain |
| Hyperparameter tuning | ↑ | ↑↑ | ↑↑ | Use AMT, early stopping |
| Early stopping | ↔ | ↓ | ↓ | May miss best model |
| Spot instances | ↔ | ↔ | ↓↓ | Requires checkpointing |
| Distributed training | ↔ | ↓↓ | ↑↑ | Data vs model parallelism |
| Smaller model | ↓ | ↓ | ↓↓ | Good for latency/cost |
| Distillation | ↓ | ↑ | ↓ | Teacher-student, needs prompts |
| Managed AI service | ↔ | ↓↓ | ↓ | For solved tasks (Textract, etc.) |
| Serverless inference | ↔ | N/A | ↓ | Cold starts; intermittent traffic |
| Provisioned throughput | ↔ | N/A | ↑ | Predictable high volume |

### FM-Specific Tradeoffs (Adjacent)
- **Prompting:** Fastest, cheapest, no training. **Start here.**
- **RAG:** Fresh, citable data; adds retrieval latency/cost; no model retraining.
- **Fine-tuning:** Domain behavior/style; training cost, versioning, **catastrophic forgetting** risk.
- **Distillation:** Smaller, cheaper, faster; some quality loss.
- **Provisioned Throughput:** Predictable latency for high volume; higher cost.
- **On-Demand:** Spiky traffic; per-token cost.

### Exam Gotchas
> **Performance metric must match business objective:** Recall for fraud, RMSE for forecast, F1 for imbalance.
> **Training time includes data prep and tuning:** Often >80% of effort. Managed services reduce it.
> **Inference cost dominates at scale:** Optimize total cost of ownership, not just training cost.
> **Interpretability is a hard requirement in finance/healthcare:** Simpler model may beat complex one.
> **Prompt before fine-tune:** Fine-tune only when evaluations show persistent gap.
> **Spot training needs checkpointing:** Otherwise interruption loses progress.
> **Serverless inference has cold starts:** Not for strict low-latency SLAs.
> **Provisioned throughput is for steady high volume:** Not cost-effective for spiky traffic.
> **Distillation uses prompts, not labels:** Teacher generates responses.
> **RAG vs Fine-tuning:** RAG for fresh/citable knowledge; fine-tuning for behavior/style.

### Security & Compliance (Cost/Time Impact)
- **KMS:** Encrypt training data, model artifacts, endpoints at rest.
- **IAM:** Execution roles for SageMaker, Bedrock; least privilege.
- **VPC:** Use VPC endpoints for private access to S3/Bedrock; no internet.
- **Audit:** CloudTrail, SageMaker Model Monitor for compliance; adds cost.

### Anti-Patterns
- **Custom model for solved task:** Use Textract, Rekognition, Comprehend, Transcribe.
- **ML for fixed logic:** Use rules.
- **Fine-tuning before prompting:** Prompt engineering first.
- **Ignoring inference cost:** Training cheap, inference expensive at scale.
- **Over-tuning on wrong metric:** Align with business KPI.
- **Real-time endpoint for batch:** Use Batch Transform.
- **Provisioned throughput for spiky traffic:** Use on-demand.
- **No checkpointing with Spot:** Lost progress.
