# About the Author

---

## About the Author: Chip Huyen
- Writer and computer scientist specializing in machine learning systems.
- Worked at NVIDIA and Snorkel AI; founded an AI infrastructure startup later acquired.
- Taught ML systems at Stanford; authored four bestselling Vietnamese books.
- *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (2022) is an Amazon AI bestseller in over 10 languages.

---

## Colophon: The Omani Owl
- Cover animal: Omani owl (*Strix butleri*), an “earless owl” native to Oman, Iran, and the UAE.
- Named after ornithologist Colonel Edward Arthur Butler, from an owl collected in 1878.
- In 2013, *Strix omanensis* was described from photographs and sound recordings, with no specimen.
- In 2015, holotype analysis showed it was the same species; the common owl became *Strix hadorami*.
