## Key Concepts in the Training Process
Training involves **epochs** (full passes over the dataset), **steps** (updates per batch), and **batch size** (samples processed before updating weights). Larger batches stabilize gradients but need more memory; smaller ones add noise that can aid generalization.

**Parameters** (weights, biases) are learned from data. **Hyperparameters** (learning rate, epochs, layers/nodes, number of trees, optimizer choice) are set by you and control the learning process. Common optimizers: Gradient Descent, SGD, Adam.

**K-fold cross-validation** uses all data for both training and validation by rotating foldsâ€”useful when data is limited.

## Regularization & Preventing Over/Underfitting
- **L1 (Lasso)**: Adds absolute weight penalty â†’ sparse models, feature selection.
- **L2 (Ridge)**: Adds squared weight penalty â†’ smaller weights, reduces multicollinearity.
- **Dropout / weight decay**: Randomly drops neurons or penalizes large weights during training.
These combat **overfitting**. Underfitting is fixed by increasing model capacity or training longer. **Catastrophic forgetting** (in transfer learning) is mitigated by lower learning rates or elastic weight consolidation. **Early stopping** halts training when validation loss rises (e.g., after epoch 15 of 20), saving cost and preventing overfitting.

## Hyperparameter Tuning & SageMaker AMT
SageMaker Automatic Model Tuning (AMT) supports:
- **Grid search**: Exhaustive categorical combinations.
- **Random search**: Random samples from ranges (efficient).
- **Bayesian optimization**: Uses prior results to pick next values.
- **Hyperband**: Allocates more epochs to promising configs and stops poor ones early.

Use AMT with built-in algorithms, script mode, or custom containers. **Script mode** lets you bring TensorFlow/PyTorch code; SageMaker handles the infrastructure.

## Improving Performance & Model Size
- **Transfer learning / fine-tuning**: Start from pre-trained models (SageMaker JumpStart or Amazon Bedrock) on your custom dataset instead of training from scratch.
- **Ensembles**: Bagging, boosting (XGBoost, CatBoost), stacking. Train multiple models and combine predictionsâ€”strong for fraud, medical, CV/NLP. Deploy as one endpoint to cut cost.
- **Reduce model size**: Pruning, quantization (change data types), feature selection, compression. Smaller models = faster inference + lower cost.
- **Distributed training**: Speeds large jobs across instances.

## Exam Tips & Traps
- **Early stopping** is the go-to for rising validation loss + cost control.
- FSx for Lustre is preferred over EFS for high-speed training data; PCA alone does not accelerate job start.
- Redshift ML keeps data in-placeâ€”do not move to S3 + SageMaker if latency/cost matter.

- Ensemble via single training/tuning job + one endpoint reduces overhead.
- Trap: Confusing parameters (learned) with hyperparameters (set by you).
