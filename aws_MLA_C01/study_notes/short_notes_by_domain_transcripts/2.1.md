## Key ML Algorithms & When to Use
Select algorithms based on problem type, data, and complexity:
- **Supervised**: Linear/logistic regression (continuous/binary outcomes, high interpretability); multinomial logistic (multi-class); SVM (classification with clear margins); decision trees/random forests (non-linear, feature importance); KNN (instance-based classification).
- **Unsupervised**: K-means (clustering); PCA (dimensionality reduction); LDA (topic modeling); word2vec (embeddings).
- **Sequence**: Seq2seq (translation/generation).
Assess data volume/complexity firstâ€”simple linear models for low complexity; trees/ensembles for tabular; avoid overkill on small datasets.

## Deep Learning Fundamentals
Neural nets use artificial neurons, weights, biases, and activation functions. 
- **CNNs**: Image classification/object detection (spatial hierarchies via convolutional layers).
- **RNNs**: Sequences/time-series (translation, sentiment, audio)â€”order matters.
**Vanishing gradient fix**: LSTMs (gates for remember/forget; accurate but slower training due to complexity) vs. GRUs (simpler gates, faster/less compute). Prefer GRU if training time is constrained.

## Model Interpretability
Trade-off: higher performance (complex models) often lowers interpretability. 
- **Intrinsic**: Built-in (linear regression = weighted sums; decision trees = if-then rules)â€”ideal for simple relationships.
- **Post-hoc**: Model-agnostic (local for single predictions; global for overall behavior). Applies to NNs. Use SageMaker Clarify (SHAP values) for bias detection and explanations.

## SageMaker for Custom Modeling
Fully managed build/train/deploy. Uses containers (for code/scripts/models/libsâ€”not scaling; deploy to managed EC2). 
- Built-in algorithms (tuned for regression, classification, etc.).
- JumpStart: Pre-trained models, solution templates, examples.

Select by cost (built-ins/FMs cheaper than custom from-scratch) and feasibility.

## Exam Tips & Traps
- Match algo to data: CNN=images, RNN/LSTM/GRU=sequences, trees=interpretability + tabular.
- AI service first for standard problems (faster/cheaper); SageMaker/JumpStart/Bedrock for custom/FMs.
- Trap: Forgetting Forecast needs training data; Polly lexicons fix pronunciation; interpretability trade-off questions favor intrinsic for simplicity.
- Cost: Prefer managed AI services/built-ins over full custom training.
- Always evaluate data feasibility before modeling.
