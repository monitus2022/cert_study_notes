### Question 1
A company is troubleshooting an error that occurs when an application attempts to create an Amazon S3 bucket. The company wants to find AWS-authored troubleshooting articles and step-by-step solutions without opening a support case. Which AWS resource should the company use?

A. AWS Knowledge Center  
B. AWS Health Dashboard  
C. AWS Marketplace  
D. AWS Trusted Advisor  

<details>
<summary>Click to view Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:**

* **Why A is correct:** The AWS Knowledge Center provides AWS-authored articles that address common technical questions, errors, and troubleshooting scenarios.
* **Why distractors are incorrect:**
  * **B (AWS Health Dashboard):** Provides information about AWS service events and account-specific health events; it is not primarily a troubleshooting article repository.
  * **C (AWS Marketplace):** Helps customers discover, purchase, and manage third-party and AWS software products.
  * **D (AWS Trusted Advisor):** Provides recommendations related to areas such as cost optimization, security, performance, and fault tolerance.

</details>
